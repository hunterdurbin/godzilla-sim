Q1 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: What do I need to play the Godzilla Card Game?
A: You’ll need a 4-card Monster Deck, a 50-card Main Deck, and around 3 to 10 Rage Cards, all made up of Godzilla Card Game cards. For first-time players, we recommend a Starter Deck, which comes with everything you need to start playing.

Q2 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: What’s the difference between a Starter Deck and a Booster Pack?
A: A Starter Deck is a pre-built deck product recommended for new players. It includes a ready-to-play deck, a playmat, and other items so you can start playing right away. Booster Packs are recommended for players who want to collect more cards or further power up their decks. Each pack contains 7 cards chosen at random from a variety of cards.

Q3 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: How many players can play the Godzilla Card Game?
A: The Godzilla Card Game is designed for 2-player battles. Outside of matches, you can also enjoy collecting your favorite cards to display.

Q4 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Is there anything I should be careful about when using card sleeves for my Monster Deck?
A: You may use different sleeves with different artwork or designs for your Monster Deck. However, make sure that holograms, logos, or other markings on the front do not cover any game-relevant information or otherwise interfere with gameplay.

Q5 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Is there anything I should be careful about when using card sleeves for my Main Deck?
A: All cards in your Main Deck must use the same type of sleeve so that individual cards cannot be distinguished. For example, sleeves that have noticeable scratches, warping, or holograms only on certain cards are not suitable. The front of the sleeve must also not cover any information needed to play the game.

Q6 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Is there anything I should be careful about when using card sleeves for my Rage Cards?
A: You may use sleeves with different artwork or designs for your Rage Cards.

Q7 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Do the sleeves for my Monster Deck, Main Deck, Rage Cards, and token cards all have to match?
A: No, they do not all need to be the same. However, all cards in your Main Deck must use the same type of sleeve so that individual cards cannot be distinguished.

Q8 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Can I use plastic protectors (such as top loaders or hard cases) on my deck?
A: Plastic protectors such as top loaders or hard cases may be used on Monster Deck cards and Rage Cards only. They cannot be used on Main Deck cards.

Q9 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Is there anything I should be careful about when shuffling my deck?
A: You must shuffle thoroughly enough that neither you nor your opponent can know or track the position of any specific card in your deck. After you shuffle, it’s best practice to allow your opponent to shuffle or cut your deck as well.

Q10 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Are there any deck-building requirements for the Monster Deck?
A: Your Monster Deck must contain exactly 4 monster cards: one monster of rank I, one of rank II, one of rank III, and one of rank IV. All 4 monster cards must be the same color as your rank I monster card.

Q11 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Are there any deck-building requirements for the Main Deck?
A: Your Main Deck must contain exactly 50 cards. All cards in the Main Deck must either be the same color as your rank I monster card in the Monster Deck, or be white. Additionally, cards with a footprint icon showing the number “2” (in the lower-right corner) may be included up to a total of 10 copies in the Main Deck.

Q12 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Can I include fewer than 9 copies of cards with a footprint value of “2”?
A: Yes. The limit is up to 10, so including 9 or fewer is completely fine.

Q13 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: How many copies of the same card can I include in my deck?
A: You may include up to 4 copies of the same card across both your Monster Deck and Main Deck combined. For example, if you place 1 copy in your Monster Deck, you may include up to 3 copies in your Main Deck.

Q14 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Does “same card” mean cards with the same card name?
A: No. “Same card” refers to cards with the same card number. Cards with identical card numbers count as the same card regardless of their name variations.

Q15 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Can I include 4 copies each of a parallel version and its original version if they share the same card number?
A: No. Parallel versions or alternate-art versions that share the same card number count as the same card. For example, EBP01-075 “Godzilla, King of the Monsters” and its parallel EBP01-075+ may be included only up to 4 copies total.

Q16 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Can I include cards with a footprint value of “2” in my Monster Deck?
A: Yes, you can.

Q17 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: If I include cards with a footprint icon of “2” in my Monster Deck, does that reduce how many I can put in my Main Deck?
A: No. Cards with a footprint icon of “2” placed in the Monster Deck do not count toward the Main Deck’s limit. You may still include up to 10 of them in your Main Deck.

Q18 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Can I include any number of monster cards in my deck?
A: Yes, you may include as many monster cards as you like. However, cards with the same card number—including the four in your Monster Deck—may total no more than 4 copies combined.

Q19 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Are all cards that depict monsters considered “Monster Cards”?
A: No. Only cards that explicitly say “Monster Card” in the upper-right corner are treated as Monster Cards under the rules.

Q20 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Can I include any number of Clash Cards in my deck?
A: Yes. You may include as many Clash Cards as you like, but cards with the same card number are limited to 4 copies total.

Q21 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Can I include any number of Strategy Cards in my deck?
A: Yes. You may include as many Strategy Cards as you like. However, cards with the same card number are limited to 4 copies total.

Q22 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Do the four cards in the Monster Deck have to be specific, predetermined cards?
A: No. Any Monster Cards may be used in your Monster Deck. You only need to match the required color and shared traits; you are not restricted to “exclusive” or preassigned monsters.

Q23 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: How many Rage Cards should I prepare?
A: There is no fixed number, but it’s recommended to prepare around 3 to 10 Rage Cards so you don’t run out during gameplay.

Q24 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Where should I place my Rage Cards during the game?
A: Place them somewhere they won’t get mixed in with your deck or discard pile— for example, next to your Monster Deck or next to your Strategy Card area.

Q25 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Do I need to reveal the contents of my Monster Deck or Main Deck to my opponent?
A: No. You do not need to reveal either deck to your opponent.

Q26 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: If I have versions of the same card with different rarities, can I treat them as separate cards and include more than four copies?
A: No. Rarity differences do not make cards count as separate cards. Cards with the same card number are treated as the same card and are limited to 4 copies total.

Q27 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: Can I mix cards from Starter Decks, Booster Set #1, Booster Set #2, and other expansions?
A: Yes. As long as the deck-building rules (such as color and card limits) are followed, you may use cards from any expansion.

Q28 (2025/06/13) Preparing for battle
Q: What does "Color" refer to?
A: When building a deck, you may only use cards of the same color. All decks may only be constructed using cards of the same color as the rank I card from your Monster Deck. However, White cards can be used in any deck. For example, if you choose red, you may construct a deck with white and red cards. Additionally, during gameplay, certain card effects may also reference colors.

Q29 (2025/06/13) During battle
Q: What happens when a deck runs out of cards?
A: Nothing happens when a deck runs out of cards. When you want to draw a card and there are no cards left in the deck, shuffle all the cards in the discard pile to form a new deck. For example, if a deck has 1 card left but an effect requires the player to draw 2 cards, that player will draw the remaining card, then form a new deck, and draw another card.

Q30 (2025/06/13) During battle
Q: Is there a limit to the maximum number of cards in a player's hand?
A: No, there is not.

Q31 (2025/06/13) During battle

Q32 (2025/06/13) During battle

Q33 (2025/06/13) During battle
Q: Does a card's ability activate regardless of which zone the card is in?
A: No. Unless otherwise specified, abilities are generally only activate when the card is in the specified zone or when a strategy card is placed in the strategy card zones. Some abilities, such as “You can play this card with its rank reduced by 4.” or “Burst” are active outside of zones.

Q34 (2025/06/13) During battle
Q: Multiple card abilities triggered at the same time. What order should they be resolved in?
A: If all card abilities belong to the same player, that player may choose the order in which they activate. If they belong to different players, the turn player will resolve their effects first.

Q: What are the conditions for using a battle card in my hand?
A: You may use a battle card if the rank of the battle card, indicated in the upper-left corner, is equal to or lower than the number of the zone in which the opponent's monster card is in. For example, if the opponent's monster card is in zone 4, you may use rank 1 to 4 battle cards.

For example, if your monster card is in zone 4, you may use rank 1 to 4 strategy cards.

Q84 (2025/06/13) During battle
Q: What can a player do during the Main Phase?
A: During the Main Phase, a player can: 1. Play battle cards from their hand 2. Use strategy cards from their hand 3. Play monster cards from their hand 4. Gain Rage by discarding monster cards from their hand 5. Advance their monster cards by discarding a card from their hand
